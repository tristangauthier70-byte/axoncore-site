Axoncore offers three independent modules — Voice, Engage, and Connect — each available in its own tiers. Voice Module — AI Phone Receptionist

An AI voice receptionist ("Riley") that answers inbound calls 24/7, including public holidays. Core capabilities include answering calls in under 3 seconds under normal operating conditions, handling appointment bookings, rescheduling, and cancellations, responding to frequently asked questions, and capturing enquiries after business hours.

Not included in the Voice module: Website chat functionality, outbound lead follow-up, WhatsApp or social media messaging automation, or any feature described under the Engage or Connect modules.

Engage Module — AI Website Concierge

An AI concierge on your website, automated lead qualification, automated follow-up email sequences (sent only to leads who have expressly opted in), and a Google Sheets lead dashboard.

Not included in the Engage module: Phone call handling, WhatsApp Business API messaging, or any feature described under the Voice or Connect modules.

Connect Module — AI WhatsApp Agent

WhatsApp Business API automation via 360dialog (a Meta-approved Business Solution Provider) — an AI agent that replies to your WhatsApp enquiries in under 3 seconds, with rich media support and a consolidated lead dashboard.

Not included in the Connect module: Phone call handling or website concierge functionality, Instagram or Facebook automation (not currently offered), or any feature described under the Voice or Engage modules.
